    Front centering across graders

    Checked live against published front centering tolerances only — back figures are shown for reference (a separate scan, generally looser) but not evaluated here, and corners, surface, and edges aren't assessed at all. A tier that centering allows is a ceiling, not a prediction: condition elsewhere can still cap the real grade lower. PSA and TAG publish exact numeric scales; CGC publishes most tiers; Beckett (BGS) has never published one exact table, so its figures are the best-corroborated community consensus, not a company document — expect more real-world variance there than the other three.
